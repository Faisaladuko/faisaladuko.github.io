Faisal (Aduko) Wahabu

I am a researcher and engineer specializing in satellite geodesy, AI-driven navigation systems, and precision hardware-software verification. Currently pursuing advanced research at Saint Louis University, I focus on bridging theoretical algorithms with real-world implementations in GNSS processing, autonomous systems, and embedded co-design.

With over four years of industry experience, I've contributed as an Automation Engineer at Supra Oracles, developing large-scale blockchain consensus simulations and multi-node testing frameworks and as a Software Engineer at General Electric, where I worked on Python-based backend systems, AI/LLM integration, and AWS cloud infrastructure.

Currently working on cutting-edge projects including secure drone mesh networks with MANET/FANET protocols and microsecond-precision GPS timing devices. Explore my research for academic contributions, portfolio for technical implementations, or download my CV.

Latest News

October 2025
Currently developing a Secure Drone Mesh Network with Dynamic Routing - implementing MANET/FANET protocols for autonomous drone swarm communication. The system features multi-hop routing with Bellman-Ford algorithm, neighbor discovery via HELLO beacons, and real-time topology adaptation for mobility. Future enhancements include AES encryption and CARLA simulator integration for 3D visualization.
October 2025
Designing an FPGA-Based GPS Timing Device with Microsecond Precision on Artix-7 FPGA using Vivado and VHDL. The hardware-software co-design features GPS-synchronized 1PPS signal processing, MicroBlaze soft processor for NMEA parsing, and microsecond-resolution pulse generation with holdover mode. Achieved sub-100 nanosecond synchronization accuracy and ~1 μs/s holdover drift performance for high-precision timing applications.
